How automated is the handoff?

When ChampHarbinger finds a qualified prospect, what happens next?

A

Fully automatic

Signal fires → contact is instantly pushed into ChampIQ → outreach workflow starts without any human step. Zero friction. Best for high-volume, high-confidence signals.

Fastest time-to-outreach Risk of low-quality leads slipping through
B

Review queue — you approve before handoff

Signal fires → prospect lands in a ChampHarbinger review queue → you (or the team) approve, reject, or edit → approved contacts are pushed to ChampIQ. Best for early signal tuning when you're still calibrating confidence.

Human quality control Signal feedback loop Adds review step to the workflow
C

Per-signal control — configure per signal type

High-confidence signals (funding round, exec hire) push automatically. Lower-confidence signals (sentiment shift, blog post) go to review queue first. Each signal has its own auto-push threshold setting.

Most flexible Scales with signal confidence More config to set up

In all cases, ChampHarbinger enriches the contact before handoff — ChampIQ receives name, verified email, title, company context, the signal that fired, ICP score, and talking points baked in.
